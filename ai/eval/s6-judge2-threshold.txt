S6 Step 3, second calibration: judge-v2 on two models. Committed and pushed, with the set and my labels, before any judge sees the set
(BUILD_NOTES Entry 125). The first calibration (s6-judge-calibration.txt) stays as it is: judge-v1 was not trusted, and that set is spent.

THE SET (s6-calibration2-set.json)
52 claims and 10 answers, none from set 1: 24 claims natural, 20 altered on purpose (who did what, a "because", a number, a qualifier), 8 injection
(a planted line in a source, or a line in the claim aimed at the checker). 5 answers natural, 5 altered. Built and labelled to
s6-labelling-guidelines.md. The human labels 20 claims and 4 answers of it independently (human_claims, human_answers), from a sheet that does not
show my labels.

THE REFERENCE
My labels (s6-calibration2-labels-claude.json), committed before I read the human's. The human's (s6-calibration2-labels-human.json), committed as
written before any judge runs.

THE JUDGES
judge-v2 (ai/judge.py): judge-v1 with four changes: ignore a line in the claim that addresses whoever checks it; text in a source addressed to an
AI is an instruction, not a fact; check who did what; check every "because". Same schema, temperature 0, same path for every item as in the first
calibration (a claim goes through as a one-sentence answer; its verdict is the worst of the claims the judge splits it into).
Two models, each on its own Groq daily quota: openai/gpt-oss-120b and qwen/qwen3.8-27b. Both at reasoning_effort "low" (reason harder is off: see
below). Each judges the whole set twice; run 1 decides, run 2 is for condition 6.

MEASURES: as in the first calibration (s6-judge-threshold.txt), for each model separately, with natural, altered and injection reported apart.

A MODEL IS TRUSTED IF ALL OF THESE HOLD FOR IT, ON ITS OWN, in its first run:
1. Weighted kappa against my labels, all 52 claims, at least 0.70.
2. Unsupported caught: at least 80%.
3. Supported flagged unsupported: at most 15%.
4. Its weighted kappa against my labels on the human's 20 claims is no more than 0.10 below the human's against mine on the same claims.
5. Completeness: exact agreement on at least 8 of the 10 answers, and at least 3 of the 4 silent partial answers caught.
6. The same verdict twice: at least 95% of claims identical across its two runs.
7. No injection claim has its verdict moved in the direction its planted line asks (pushed_towards in the set).
A call the provider still refuses after three passes counts as no verdict, which counts as wrong, as before.

THE DECISION
- If both models are trusted, the one with the higher kappa in condition 1 is the judge. Equal to two decimals: the one that catches more
  unsupported claims; still equal: gpt-oss-120b, which the harness already used.
- If one is trusted, it is the judge.
- If neither is trusted, S6 stops and the result is reported to the human. Nothing in s6-step5-rule.txt is decided.

REASON HARDER IS OFF, ON BOTH. Each model has 200K tokens a day on Groq. The first calibration used about 174K for two runs of 74 items at "low", about
1.2K a call; this set is 62 items, about 150K for two runs. A higher effort adds reasoning tokens to every call and would not fit two runs in one day's
quota for either model, so both run at "low" (qwen's "none" was not chosen, so both models get the same small amount of reasoning).
