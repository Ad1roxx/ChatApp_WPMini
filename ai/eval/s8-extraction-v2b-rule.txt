S8 extraction v2, re-measured with the cut-off fix: the decision rule. Decided by the human 2026-11-13, committed and pushed
before extraction v2 is restored and before any run of it (BUILD_NOTES Entry 147). Gemini only.

WHY
Extraction v2 (s8-extraction-v2-rule.txt) reached R8 11/11 in its run 1 but was not adopted: two verdict calls were cut off at the
output cap, which failed conditions 2 and 6 (Entry 144). The cut-off is now fixed in the product (Entry 145: three criteria a
verdict call, and a reply cut off is asked again half at a time), and condition 6 is amended for future runs (Entry 146: a pair
with a failed call on either side is not comparable). This re-measures v2 with both in place.

WHAT IS MEASURED
Exactly extraction v2 as built in Entry 143: (a) a PDF rubric's own page text outside its tables; (b) the two prompt lines
(criteria-v2). Restored unchanged, with its tests. Plus what is now in the product: the cut-off fix. No label, case, other
prompt or condition changes; R8 is not relabelled.

HOW IT IS MEASURED
The whole set, two runs from scratch with the paced harness, saved as s8-x2b-run1.json and s8-x2b-run2.json and scored from those
files (s8-x2b-report.txt). Run 1 decides; run 2 is reported beside it. Replies cut off at the output cap are counted and
reported (the "after" figure for Entry 145). The human's original PDFs run too, reported apart, not part of any condition.
It may run across two days' quota.

THE BASELINE: the scored run 1 (s8-run1.json), as in s8-extraction-v2-rule.txt
Extraction per case: B1 4/4, B2 3/3, B3 5/5, R1 5/5, R2 5/5, R3 5/5, R4 5/5, R5 5/5, R6 4/4, R7 5/5, R8 1/11, R9 3/4; whole set 50/61.
Condition 2: 44/61. Condition 3: 0/12. Condition 4: 1/23. Condition 5: 14/14. Condition 6: 1 failing (scored as amended, the
same: no pair in s8-run1.json has a failed call).

EXTRACTION V2 IS ADOPTED IF ALL OF THESE HOLD IN RUN 1:
1. R8: at least 9 of 11 hand-made criteria found.
2. No other case's extraction is lower than its baseline above.
3. The whole set: at least 90% found, i.e. at least 55 of 61.
4. Conditions 2 to 6 of s8-threshold.txt no worse than the baseline: condition 2 at least 44/61; condition 3 at most 0;
   condition 4 at most 1; condition 5 14/14; condition 6 (as amended) at most 1 failing.
A call that fails after its retries is a wrong verdict, as in s8-threshold.txt.

IF ADOPTED
Extraction v2 stays. The new run 1 is reported against s8-threshold.txt as S8's current result; S8 stays behind its off-by-default
experimental setting unless all six of that rule's conditions hold.
IF NOT
Extraction v2 is reverted again; the runs are kept and reported, and S8's result stays the one in s8-report.txt. The cut-off fix
stays either way: it is measured here, not decided here.
Nothing here is loosened after measuring.
